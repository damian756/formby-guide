Sorrento Restaurant
BENVENUTI
Sorrento brings a little piece of Italy to Formby village, our friendly staff make the experience even more interesting, find out what part of Italy they are from and hear their stories, it really will transport you to Italy itself.  We are perfect for families, parties and celebrations, whilst still creating an intimate date night dining experience.
Embark on the full Sorrento journey in traditional Italian style settings and atmosphere, or simply indulge in a refreshing aperol spritz accompanied with an  antipasto in the sunshine in our lovely outdoor seating area.
We always deliver freshly prepared food, second to none customer service and create a lively, uplifting atmosphere.
AL FRESCO
We have turned our outdoor area into a stylish Mediterranean inspired space, f illed with beautiful flowers and surrounding . We have put our all into making your outdoor dining experience with us something to remember.
