Ribble & Alt Estuaries SPA. Description: lies on the coast of Lancashire and Sefton in northwest England. Overlaps the Ribble Estuary SSSI and Sefton Coast SSSI. Extensive sandflats and mudflats, large areas of saltmarsh, coastal grazing marsh.

Qualifying features: breeding ruff, common tern, lesser black-backed gull. Wintering Bewick's swan, whooper swan, golden plover, bar-tailed godwit, pink-footed goose, shelduck, wigeon, teal, pintail, oystercatcher, grey plover, knot, sanderling, dunlin, black-tailed godwit, redshank. Passage ringed plover, sanderling, redshank. Waterbird and seabird assemblages.

Stakeholder concerns: "Beach recreation disturbs roosting flocks of birds and ground nesting birds on the coast." Motorsports, low-flying microlights. "All these activities have an impact on bird energetics and their chances of survival, especially in winter."
