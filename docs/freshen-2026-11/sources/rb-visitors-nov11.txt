Buggies
Because of a disability, certain guests might require assistance from a motorised ride-on buggy to play golf.
Regrettably, due to the terrain conditions on the Links, visitors are not permitted to bring their own buggies. Please reach out to the office to explore alternative options for your golfing experience.
We are unable to accept advanced bookings for buggies prior to 1:00pm. However, if buggies are available on the day of play, they may be hired by golfers who present a valid medical certificate. Availability is subject to demand, and we recommend checking with the Pro Shop on arrival.

Dress Code
Dress Code
Royal Birkdale is a traditional members’ Club. We ask all members, guests and visitors to dress smartly at all times, with attire that reflects the culture of the Club.
Smart golfing attire is required on the course, including a collared shirt tucked in and dimple or textured-soled golf shoes. Tailored shorts are permitted with ankle socks. Jeans, tracksuits, round-neck T-shirts and training shoes are not permitted.
In the Clubhouse, golf attire is acceptable on the Ground Floor. All headwear must be removed upon entering the Clubhouse.

Tee Selection
We offer four scaled tee options, ranging from 4,877 to 6,736 yards. Our tee selection chart will guide you to the most suitable choice for your game. For the most authentic experience, we recommend selecting the tee that reflects the course as designed by the architect—presenting the same strategic challenges faced by professionals during Championships. To maintain the quality of play, those wishing to play the longest tees must seek approval from the Office.
Slow Play
Normal courtesies and etiquette for golf should be always observed. If your group is holding up play, please stand aside and let the group behind play through.
Ready Golf
At Royal Birkdale ‘Ready Golf’ is encouraged, players are both allowed and encouraged to play out of turn in a safe and responsible way.

Terms & Conditions
Terms & Conditions
Online Bookings & Groups of 8 and below
Full payment of the green fee is required at the time of booking to secure the tee time.
Booking Conditions & Groups of 9 and more
A deposit of 50% of the total green fee is required to secure the booking. A provisional enquiry will be held for 14 days, or until 60 days before the date of play if that is sooner, after which the tee time will be released unless the deposit has been paid. The balance of the green fee is due no later than 60 days before the date of play. Bookings made 60 days or fewer before the date of play must be paid in full at the time of booking.
Reduction in Numbers
Groups can reduce in numbers up to 60 days before the day of play with no penalty. The total deposit paid will be applied to the balance for the remaining players.
Refunds & Cancellations
Cancellations received more than 60 days before the date of play will result in forfeiture of the deposit. For groups of 8 or fewer, where the green fee has been paid in full at the time of booking, 50% of the green fee is treated as the deposit. Cancellations received 60 days or fewer before the date of play will result in forfeiture of the full green fee, regardless of group size. We regret that we cannot offer refunds or future credit due to delayed/cancelled flights, travel plans, personal injury, or illness. For this reason, we strongly recommend that you have travel insurance to cover this eventuality.
Alteration of Booking
Up to 60 days before the day of play, tee times can be moved to an alternative date within a year of the original booking (subject to availability). Should the green fee rate increase the difference will need to be paid.
Adverse Weather
If you are unable to complete your round of golf due to the course closure, you will be entitled to re-book or receive a full refund for the part of the round unplayed. If the course is open and you decide not to play no refund will be given.
TEE TIME ARRANGEMENTS
Please note that when making a booking, the tee time arrangements are such that you may be playing behind or ahead of golfers in 4-ball groups. It is important to understand this when booking, as it may affect the pace of play and overall experience on the course.
WINTER & SHOULDER RATES
Please be aware that our golf course may be subject to necessary maintenance procedures during the winter and shoulder rate seasons. While we strive to minimise disruptions, some maintenance activities may temporarily impact the condition of certain areas of the course.
ACTIVE TEE TIME FILLING
We actively fill tee times to ensure efficient use of the course. Any bookings made for 2-ball or 3-ball groups may be paired with other golfers, including singles, to create 4-ball groups wherever possible. This policy is designed to enhance the pace of play and optimise the golfing experience for all players. By making a booking, you acknowledge and accept this arrangement. Due to this, we reserve the discretion to move tee times by up to 30 minutes to facilitate the efficient management of the tee sheet. Any such changes will be communicated to you in advance.
PHOTOGRAPHY & FILMING
Guests are welcome to take personal photographs and videos during their round for private, non-commercial use, such as recording personal memories of their visit. We ask that when doing so, you remain mindful of the privacy and enjoyment of other guests on the course. Any photography or filming intended for commercial purposes, including but not limited to advertisements, brand promotions, sponsored content, monetised social media or video platforms (such as YouTube or Instagram), or any activity where the individual or organisation is being financially compensated is strictly prohibited without prior written consent from Royal Birkdale. This applies regardless of the equipment used and includes, but is not limited to, drone footage, professional camera equipment, or the use of additional crew or personnel. Any footage featuring the course name, logo, signage, or branding in a commercial context may not be published or distributed without prior written approval. Guests wishing to film content at the course are encouraged to submit a filming request to the secretary’s office in advance of their visit. We welcome legitimate filming enquiries and will endeavour to accommodate requests where possible. Breach of this policy may result in removal from the course without refund, and Royal Birkdale reserves the right to pursue appropriate legal remedies where footage is used without consent.
ALCOHOL POLICY
Royal Birkdale operates a licensed premises and alcoholic beverages are available for purchase at the clubhouse and, where applicable, on the course. Guests are not permitted to bring their own alcohol onto the course or clubhouse premises. Guests are expected to drink responsibly throughout their visit. The Club reserves the right to refuse service of alcohol to any guest who appears to be intoxicated, and to remove from the premises any guest whose behaviour, whether related to alcohol consumption or otherwise, is deemed to be disruptive, offensive, or detrimental to the enjoyment of other guests. Removal from the course or premises as a result of alcohol-related behaviour will not entitle the guest to a refund of green fees or any other charges paid.
PERSONAL LIABILITY
Guests visit and play at Royal Birkdale at their own risk. The Club accepts no liability for any personal injury, loss, theft, or damage to personal property sustained on the course or premises, except where such injury, loss, or damage is caused by the proven negligence of Royal Birkdale or its employees. Guests are reminded that golf is a sport that carries inherent risks, including the risk of injury from golf balls, equipment, and the natural terrain of the course. All guests are expected to take reasonable care for their own safety and the safety of others at all times during their visit. Royal Birkdale strongly recommends that all guests hold appropriate personal insurance to cover injury, loss, or damage during their visit. For visiting groups, we recommend that an appropriate level of travel or activity insurance is arranged prior to the date of play.
