FAQ's
Please find below some of our customers Frequently Asked Questions! If you are still unsure about anything, you can get in touch with our team at Bookings@EmilysFormby.com.
Dress Code
Our dress code is relaxed during breakfast service, and smart casual during our evening service. No gym wear or tracksuits are permitted after 5pm, but trainers are allowed.
Dogs
Well behaved dogs of all sizes are more than welcome every day until 5pm. We politely requested that dogs remain off the furniture, and ask guests to ensure their dog doesn’t disrupt other guests whilst dining.
Dogs are not permitted into Emily’s during our evening service, which is after 5:00pm.
Large Groups
Due to the nature of our venue, and the service we strive to provide, we can only accommodate large groups up to 12 guests. All groups of 10 and above will be required to provide a per-order prior to their reservation.
Sunday Roast
Our Sunday Roast Menu is served every Sunday from 2:00pm – 7:00pm. Our menu is subject to change weekly to include the freshest of ingredients which is why it is not displayed on our website. For more information, please get in touch!
Walk-Ins
Walk-ins are always welcome on a first come, first served basis, but we strongly advise booking in advance to avoid disappointment, especially over weekends.
Waiting Lists
We do not currently operate a waiting list, but you can see live, up-to-date availability on our website – Any cancelled tables are made available online immediately after cancellation.
